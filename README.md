# Hi, I'm Prithviraj

I'm an Artificial Intelligence undergraduate at **Mahindra University, École Centrale School of Engineering**. I work on machine learning research and build applications around it, with a particular interest in explainability, computer vision, and language models.

A lot of my projects start with a question: where did this answer come from, why did the model make that prediction, or what can the match data tell us?

## Research & experiments

| Project | What I'm working on |
| :--- | :--- |
| **[ScholAR](https://github.com/prithvi-kaizen/ScholAR)** | A local research assistant that links generated citations to the exact paragraph, table, or figure in a PDF. Hybrid retrieval, local models, and inspectable evidence traces. |
| **[KAD](https://github.com/prithvi-kaizen/KAD)** | Studying causal gradient starvation in recurrent networks: when learning a dominant feature slows a weaker one. Paired experiments, finite-width analysis, and mitigation studies. |

## Football, through data

Football keeps finding its way into my code.

- **[AthleteLoad](https://github.com/prithvi-kaizen/Athlete-Load):** explainable injury-risk modeling from football training and wellness data, with TreeSHAP attributions and an interactive dashboard.
- **[Expected goals from scratch](https://github.com/prithvi-kaizen/FOOTBALL-DATA-ANALYSIS/tree/main/expected-goals-model):** shot geometry, logistic regression, and XGBoost on StatsBomb open data, with calibration checks and a benchmark against StatsBomb xG.
- **[World Cup 2026 Predictor](https://github.com/prithvi-kaizen/fifa-wc-26-predictor):** match probabilities using Elo ratings, a Dixon-Coles corrected Poisson model, and XGBoost.
- **[Barça UCL](https://github.com/prithvi-kaizen/barca-ucl):** an interactive look at Barcelona's five European Cup and Champions League winning campaigns.
